# FullStack VC — Agentic VC Dojo

> {{SITE_NAME}}

FullStack VC is the public hub of the **Agentic VC Dojo** — a working & learning community of active venture professionals practicing how to make agentic AI a daily craft inside their firms. The Dojo is incubated by the Kauffman Fellows AI SIG and runs as monthly web meetings, speed demos, setupathons, and a long-form Venture Handbook the community drafts in public.

This site is **not a venture capital firm**. It is an information and organization hub: session pages, presenter and participant directories, a working tools registry, the Venture Handbook chapters, working groups, and projects.
