The kickoff session for the Agentic VC Dojo. We'll set the table for the year:
what "agentic" actually means in a VC firm context, the practices we've seen
work (and the ones we've seen fail), and how members can get the most out of
each monthly session.

## Format

- 10 min — framing: the year ahead
- 25 min — workshop: bring an AI workflow you're trying to operationalize
- 20 min — open Q&A
- 5 min  — what's next + next month's preview

## Who this is for

VCs from analyst to partner. No prior AI engineering background required.
You'll get more out of the sessions if you come with a real workflow you're
trying to improve, but pure observers are welcome too.
