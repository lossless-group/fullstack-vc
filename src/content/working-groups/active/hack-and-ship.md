## Why this exists

A surprising number of VCs were software people first. The instinct to build doesn't go away when you start writing checks — but the time and the *permission* often do. Hack & Ship is the permission. A standing place where building something small and real is the first-order activity, not a hobby tucked between meetings.

The bet is that VCs who keep building stay sharper diligence partners, faster portfolio collaborators, and more credible advocates for the founders they back.

## What we share

- Build show-and-tells — short demos of whatever's in flight.
- Shippable artifacts — internal tools, scripts, prototypes, tiny SaaS — anything that solves something specific.
- Project work — currently centered on **MemoPop AI** (investment memos drafted alongside diligence rather than after).

VCs (or VC-adjacent platform people) who want to maintain a building practice. No qualification required beyond willingness to ship something — even something small — and show it.
