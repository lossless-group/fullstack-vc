## Why this exists

Every VC firm is awash in information — meeting notes, market data & reports, portfolio updates, deal flow datarooms, CRM and Portfolio data, newsletters, events, content & media exposure, and more. Most all professionals can only barely make sense of it in their head -- pattern recognition. Information from separate channels, data in silos, files scattered and rarely opened. _How can we create more value from the data we already have?_, _How can we channel that information into better decisions?_ 

Data-Driven Venture WG works out the _how_ together: how to have Research Agents, parse market research PDFs, generate meaningful visualizations from disconnected spreadsheets and financiels, automate email update forwarding and ingestion into Database Apps (like Airtable), and more.

## What we share

- Real workflows (sanitized) — the actual channels, materials, file systems, apps, and tools we use.
- Unembarrased unknowns — what we're still figuring out, what's not working, what we're hacking around.
- Vendor, tech stack, app, and tool evaluations from members who've kicked the tires.
- Case studies and lessons learned from failed experiments.
- Motivation to take anxiety and uncertainty to a productive inquiry.
- Active project work.

## Who this is for

VCs and platform teams who want investing-as-information-processing, portfolio support to be targetted, materials to be accurate and insightful -- all to be a *practice* rather than a one-off hustle. Bring real data (sanitized as needed) or information channels with an intent to build a system.
