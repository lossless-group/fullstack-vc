Founder of Aire Labs, which builds custom AI models, machine-learning workflows, and geospatial visualizations for teams leading energy-transition and carbon-removal projects. Kauffman Fellow, Class 18.

A rare operator-investor-builder triple: co-founded Kindred (AI robotics, acquired by Ocado for $330M), Rypple (enterprise software, acquired by Salesforce for $65M), and Sweat Equity Ventures. Started at BlackBerry, then served as a Director at OMERS Ventures — where he did his fellowship under John Ruffolo — working on investments including Shopify and Wattpad.

Three degrees from the University of Toronto (Engineering Science, JD, MBA), product management at Berkeley Haas, a registered Canadian patent agent with two patents of his own, and — when not shipping models — training for an Ironman.
