Post-doc educated investor and complexity scientist, with a decade in academia and 11+ years in venture capital. Francesco started his VC career at Anthemis, went on to lead the data function at Balderton Capital and then Greycroft, and has advised funds including Kleiner Perkins, Owl Ventures, and Apis on data and AI strategy.

He specializes in alternative data and AI applied to private markets — the question of what a fund can actually learn from its own data — with a personal portfolio spanning early-stage deeptech, dev tools, healthcare, and climate. His academic output includes 5 books and 20+ peer-reviewed papers. Based in the United Kingdom.
