Founder of Prose Ventures and Kauffman Fellow, Class 22, where she co-leads the Blockchain SIG. Jules is an entrepreneur, investor, and corporate-innovation executive — currently incubating an agentic AI + blockchain startup in the creator economy and investing in startups and funds through Prose Ventures.

Earlier, she co-founded and led two venture-backed legal-tech startups, both acquired. BA from UCLA, MSc from the London School of Economics. Splits her time between Nashville and NYC.
