The prompt engine behind the Dojo's terminal-setup recipe. Works with zsh, bash, fish, and PowerShell; themes are plain JSON, so customizing means deleting or recoloring segments — no shell scripting required.

See it in use: [[guides/terminal-setup-ghostty-oh-my-posh/index|How To: Getting Unafraid and Setting Up Your Terminal]].
