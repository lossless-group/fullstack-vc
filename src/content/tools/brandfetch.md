Brandfetch resolves a domain to a structured brand bundle — logos in multiple formats and modes, colors, fonts, and metadata — via a single API call. In the Dojo it's the go-to for filling in portfolio company and presenter affiliations without manually downloading and renaming logo assets per firm.

Common pairings: dealflow enrichment pipelines, slide-deck building, OG-image generation for participant cards.
