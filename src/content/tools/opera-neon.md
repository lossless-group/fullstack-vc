Neon is Opera's entry, and the distinguishing fact is the provenance: this is a company that has shipped a browser continuously since 1995, arriving at agentic features from the browser side rather than the model side. Everyone else in this category is an AI company that built a browser. If your concern is that a nine-month-old startup is now the thing standing between your logins and the open web, that difference is not nothing.

Positioning is squarely on **action** — Neon is built to complete tasks rather than to summarize the page you're reading, which puts it closer to [[tools/comet|Comet]] and [[tools/aside|Aside]] than to [[tools/dia-browser|Dia]]. The agentic capability is a paid tier, at roughly $19.90/month, which is the most expensive entry point in the category and worth weighing against Comet being free.

For Dojo purposes it's a credible option rather than a first recommendation — try Comet or Aside first, and reach for Neon if vendor longevity is the axis you're optimizing. Context in [[guides/agent-native-browsers/index|Orientation: Agent-Native Browsers]].
