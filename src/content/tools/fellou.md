Fellou's bet is that the thing blocking adoption isn't capability, it's **legibility**. Every other browser in this category takes your instruction and starts acting; you find out what it decided to do by watching it happen. Fellou renders the plan first — a visual, inspectable, editable workflow — and only executes once you've looked at it. You can delete a step, change one, or stop it partway through.

For venture that maps onto a specific and recurring problem. The tasks worth automating are usually the ones touching material you have obligations about: portfolio-company correspondence, LP communications, cap-table documents. "Watch the first run of any new task" is the standing rule in [[guides/agent-native-browsers/index|Orientation: Agent-Native Browsers]], and Fellou is the product that makes that rule a feature rather than a discipline you have to remember.

It's also the most defensible answer when a partner or counsel asks what exactly the agent is allowed to do — you can show them, before it runs. Worth evaluating alongside [[tools/sigma-browser|Sigma]] if the blocker at your firm is governance rather than capability, and against [[tools/comet|Comet]] or [[tools/aside|Aside]] if it's raw task success.
