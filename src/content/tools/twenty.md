Twenty is the CRM the Dojo keeps coming back to when the question is "which CRM works with agents?" Because you can own the database and the API, Claude can create and update records for you: the "tell Claude what to do instead of logging into the CRM" workflow.

In the group: one member runs several Twenty instances (personal plus small funds), and another is moving a large PE client off Excel into Twenty with Claude as the interface. A third will try it "if it's AI native". Compare [[tools/affinity|Affinity]] (VC-specific, hosted), [[tools/hubspot|HubSpot]] and [[tools/lightfield|Lightfield]].
