OpenRouter is the reference point for the whole category: when a tool says it's "like OpenRouter, but…", this is what it means. One key gets you nearly every model, which is why so many agent tools (and the people who build their own) default to it for trying models side by side or switching when a provider has an outage.

The "but…" is usually about trust and control. The alternatives in the registry each pick one concern and go deeper:

- [[tools/trustedrouter|TrustedRouter]]: no-logging you can verify through attested hardware, not just a policy.
- [[tools/concentrate-ai|Concentrate.ai]]: compliance done once (HIPAA under a single BAA) and pooled pricing.
- [[tools/requesty|Requesty]]: governance — spend caps per key, PII masking, audit logs, data kept in-region.

For a fund: OpenRouter is fine for experimenting and for non-sensitive work. Once diligence notes, LP data or portfolio financials go through a model API, check its data-policy settings, or pick one of the alternatives above.
