Cowork brings the [[tools/claude-code|Claude Code]] idea — an agent that works on your actual files — to people who will never open a terminal. For most fellows it's the shortest path from "chatting with Claude" to "Claude did the work."

A Dojo member pairs it with [[tools/town|Town]]: Town handles the personal web errands and email, Cowork does the document work. Several fellows in Microsoft shops are asking how well it runs on Windows and alongside Office — a good question to bring to a session. See also [[tools/claude-desktop|Claude Desktop]].
