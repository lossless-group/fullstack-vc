The "OpenRouter for regulated companies" pitch: same idea of one key and many models, but with the compliance paperwork done once. Signing a single BAA with Concentrate instead of one with every model provider is the practical win for health-adjacent portfolio companies, and pooled rates mean small teams get prices they couldn't negotiate alone.

A Dojo member put it on their "check out soon" list in the July Checkup. Compare [[tools/trustedrouter|TrustedRouter]] (privacy you can verify through attested hardware) and [[tools/requesty|Requesty]] (the widest model catalog, with PII masking).
