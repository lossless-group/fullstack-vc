Hyprland is the desktop layer: [[tools/omarchy|Omarchy]] ships it on Arch, and a Dojo member runs it on [[tools/nixos|NixOS]]. A tiling, keyboard-first desktop suits people who spend the day supervising agents across terminals; see the multiplexers [[tools/herdr|Herdr]], [[tools/rex|Rex]] and [[tools/tuios|TuiOS]].
