Tabbit's angle is **model choice inside the browser**. It ships with ten-plus LLMs available natively, so the model becomes a per-task decision — a cheap fast one for form filling, a frontier one for the reasoning-heavy extraction — without maintaining separate subscriptions or moving between apps. Everyone else in the category either picks a model for you ([[tools/comet|Comet]]) or asks you to bring your own ([[tools/aside|Aside]]).

It comes out of GN06, an internal research group at Meituan, and reached V1.0 in June 2026 after a long public beta. That provenance is worth naming plainly for a US-based firm evaluating it for confidential work: know where inference runs and under which jurisdiction before pointing it at portfolio-company material, per the data-handling rule in [[guides/agent-native-browsers/index|Orientation: Agent-Native Browsers]].

Most interesting as a benchmark of how fast this category is commoditizing — multi-model support arriving as a shipped feature within a year of the category existing at all.
