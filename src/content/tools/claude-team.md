Claude Team is the team-tier plan for the Claude consumer surface (claude.ai) — shared member seats with admin controls, central billing, and higher usage limits than individual Pro accounts. Sits between **Claude Pro** (single-user) and **Claude Enterprise** (SSO, audit logs, longer context, advanced admin).

For Dojo-relevant use cases:

- **Administrative & audit prep.** Shared Projects let a finance / ops team co-curate the same Claude context (financial controls, audit checklists, LP-correspondence templates) without each person rebuilding it locally.
- **Onboarding & training.** New seats inherit team-level Projects and conversation patterns immediately — useful for getting analysts productive on Claude-native workflows in days, not weeks.
- **Central billing & seat management.** One invoice, one admin, no chasing reimbursements for individual Pro subs across a small fund.

**Distinct from Claude Code** (the CLI coding agent) and **the Claude API** (programmatic access). Team seats grant access to claude.ai, the consumer surface — not API quotas. Teams that need both typically pair Claude Team for analyst/operator workflows with Claude API keys for engineering automation.

Mike Moradi & Raeed Zainuddin's May 27 All-Hands demo covers exactly this — wiring Claude Team into administrative + audit-prep workflows at Cortado Ventures.
