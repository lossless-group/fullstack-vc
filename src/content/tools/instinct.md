Instinct hides all the agent machinery behind a phone number. There's no app to learn — you text it like a person, and it acts on your email, calendar and the web. That simplicity is why it spread through the Dojo faster than anything else this fall.

**What Dojo members report.** The strongest stories are about proactivity: an unprompted message noticing that a child's camp kit needed packing before an early bus, and one member calling it "the best EA I've ever had" for travel, meetings and to-dos. The complaints are just as consistent: it's single-threaded, it loses context once a conversation scrolls away, and at least one member found it "little better than a skin on my Claude work." Another ran the same requests through Instinct and [[tools/muse|Muse]] for weeks and preferred Muse's replies.

**Personal, not work.** The question the group keeps returning to is what happens when an agent that never forgets sits between you and your firm's email. Treat it as a personal assistant first; see [[guides/personal-agents-in-the-dojo/index|Takeaways from Using Personal Agents]]. Compare with [[tools/town|Town]], [[tools/grok-bot|Grok Bot]] and [[tools/openclaw|OpenClaw]].
