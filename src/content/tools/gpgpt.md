AI co-pilot purpose-built for venture and private equity workflows. Two modules: **GPPortfolio** ingests a firm's proprietary documents — pitch decks, board decks, IC memos — and answers questions across them, surfacing cross-portfolio patterns; **GPInsights** provides market benchmarks, valuation guidance, and portfolio-construction strategy. The differentiator is grounding: responses cite the firm's own uploaded material rather than public-internet data, and customer data is never used for model training.

Founded by [[jacob-mullins]] (Village Global / Shasta Ventures). Fits the IC-memo prep, deck triage, and portfolio-monitoring patterns relevant to GPs and investment teams.
