The privacy-conscious answer to [[tools/granola|Granola]]: transcription happens on your Mac, and the transcripts are files your agents can read, rather than notes locked inside someone else's app.

A Dojo member listed it in the July Checkup as the tool that replaced both Granola and Wispr Flow for them. For a VC, the appeal is simple: partner meetings and founder calls never leave the machine unless you choose to send them somewhere.
