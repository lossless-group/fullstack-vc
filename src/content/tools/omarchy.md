Omarchy is Arch with someone else making the endless decisions for you, and it's the reason a lot of developers tried desktop Linux this year. The pitch is taste: a fast, good-looking, keyboard-first workstation where agents and terminals are first-class rather than bolted on.

For the Dojo it belongs on the power-user end of the map, next to the multiplexers. If your day is increasingly spent supervising agents in terminals — [[tools/herdr|Herdr]], [[tools/rex|Rex]], [[tools/tuios|TuiOS]] — a tiling, keyboard-driven desktop is the natural home for it. Its community is currently debating whether TuiOS should replace tmux as the default. At least two Dojo members run it day to day. Most fellows on a Mac don't need it; it's the thing to try on a spare machine.
