Email is where most of a VC's week arrives: founder updates, intros, LP questions, deal flow. Dove's bet is that an inbox should triage itself, putting the handful of messages that need you in front and letting the rest wait, across every account at once.

It's a lighter-weight alternative to handing your inbox to a full personal agent like [[tools/instinct|Instinct]] or [[tools/town|Town]]: the AI works inside an email client you still drive, rather than acting on your behalf.
