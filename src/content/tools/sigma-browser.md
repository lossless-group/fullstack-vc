Sigma's differentiator is where the inference happens. Its assistant runs **locally by default**, which changes the answer to the question that decides whether a firm can actually use one of these: *when the agent reads this page, where does the page go?*

That question is not academic in venture. Portfolio-company correspondence, LP communications, and anything under an NDA carry obligations that don't evaporate because the tool is convenient. [[guides/agent-native-browsers/index|Orientation: Agent-Native Browsers]] makes the point that you should know the answer before the first export, not after — and Sigma, along with [[tools/aside|Aside]]'s on-device memory, exists because that question has real and differing answers across the category.

The trade is the usual one: local models are less capable than frontier models, so expect a lower ceiling on complex multi-step tasks than [[tools/comet|Comet]] or Aside will give you. Good fit for the well-defined, repetitive, confidential jobs — form filling, data entry, extraction from a known layout — rather than open-ended agentic work.
