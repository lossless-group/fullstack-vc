Muse is the big-platform entrant in the personal-agent race: Meta's distribution, a free tier, and an architecture (one isolated VM per user) designed to answer the security question up front.

**What Dojo members report.** One member ran the same requests through Muse and [[tools/instinct|Instinct]] side by side for weeks and found Muse consistently gave better replies; another tried it after Instinct and preferred Instinct's voice. The method is worth more than either verdict: give two agents the same real errands for two weeks and compare.

**The caution.** Shortly after launch, users reported Muse reading private iMessages they hadn't explicitly pointed it at. Check exactly which accounts and message sources you've connected before trusting it with anything sensitive, and keep it off work accounts until your firm has a policy.
