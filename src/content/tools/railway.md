Where [[tools/vercel|Vercel]] shines for websites and front ends, Railway is the easy home for everything that needs to *keep running*: an agent's backend, a database, a scheduled job, a self-hosted open-source tool like [[tools/twenty|Twenty]] or [[tools/n8n|n8n]]. You point it at a repo and it figures out the rest.

That makes it a natural place to host a fund's own small stack: a CRM, an MCP server, a background worker that summarizes new files every night. Compare [[tools/render|Render]], and see [[guides/home-connector-on-cloudflare-artifacts/index|Build a Home Connector on Cloudflare Artifacts]] for the serverless alternative.
