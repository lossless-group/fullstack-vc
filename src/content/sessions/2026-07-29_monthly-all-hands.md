This month's Agentic VC Dojo is **The Checkup** — no demos, no presenters. Three sessions of workflows and second brains have gone by; today we find out what actually landed, where everyone's stuck, and what the dojo should do next.

📊 **[Open the session deck →](/slides/2026-07-29_monthly-all-hands)** — the live polls are embedded directly in the slides this time. Open the deck on your own screen, log in, and answer right on the slide.

## The Checkup — five questions, in your own words

1. **Recent wins** — improved workflows, adoption traction with your team, things that finally clicked.
2. **Recent intrigue** — aspirational tools, your "check out soon" list, what you've imagined improving.
3. **Rabbit holes** — yak shaving, dead-ends, where the time went. No shame; dead-ends are teaching material.
4. **The time eater** — the one thing that eats your time that AI/agents should assist with.
5. **The breakthrough** — the thing you never get around to, but an Agentic Associate doing it would change your practice.

## Then: Set the Agenda breakouts

Random groups of 4–5, twelve minutes, one shared one-pager. The five-question rail and the group page live at **[/breakouts/set-the-agenda](/breakouts/set-the-agenda)**. Report-backs get ranked live — the top-voted proposals become the August and September sessions.
