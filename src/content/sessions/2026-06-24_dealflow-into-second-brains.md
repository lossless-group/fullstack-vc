This month's Agentic VC Dojo is themed **Dealflow into Second Brains** — getting the messy front of the funnel (intake forms, inbound, sourcing notes, founder calls) into a system that compounds instead of rotting in an inbox.

A primer up front, then Marcus and Toby return to give the demos they had queued up for earlier sessions but didn't get to present:

- **Michael Staton (FullStack VC)** — Obsidian & Markdown Notebooks for Dummies. The ground-floor primer: what a markdown second brain is and how to start one.
- **Marcus Brotman (Launch)** — Intake forms + OpenClaw + Notion for better deal-pipeline insights.
- **Toby Rush (Ideem)** — OpenClaw + Obsidian for deal triage and vigilant pipeline management.

Two takes on the same problem — one routing into **Notion**, one into **Obsidian** — bookended by a from-scratch primer, so you can see how the second-brain choice shapes the workflow whether you're starting today or already deep in.

VCs from analyst to partner. No prior AI experience required — whether your dealflow lives in a spreadsheet, a CRM, or your head, come see how others are wiring it into a second brain.
