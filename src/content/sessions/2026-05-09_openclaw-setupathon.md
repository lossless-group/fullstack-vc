Join us for an OpenClaw Setupathon support group as I — and everyone else on this call — attempts to set up OpenClaw for the first time in a safe and non-judgemental setting. :-)

> **Note:** this is *not* a teaching session. It's a *support* session. We will all be trying to set up on our own different environments, hardware, or cloud, etc. People will be on hand to ask questions to, but this is not intended to be a guided setup — because I have no idea what I'm doing either. Enjoy!

## When

Saturday, May 9, 2026 · 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM (Central Time — Chicago)

## Who this is for

Anyone who has been meaning to get OpenClaw running locally (or in the cloud) and would rather flail in good company than flail alone. Bring your laptop, your half-read docs, your unresolved environment variables, and your willingness to admit when something doesn't work.

## What to bring

- A machine you intend to install OpenClaw on (or cloud credentials you intend to use).
- The OpenClaw docs open in a tab.
- A second tab for the inevitable error message you're about to paste into a search bar.
- Patience, snacks, and a sense of humor.

## What this is *not*

- A guided tutorial.
- A polished demo.
- A place where anyone is expected to know what they're doing.

See you Saturday.
