## Pitch

Most VCs run their dealflow inside their inbox and a half-maintained Notion page. The good ones have a personal operating system — a documented flow that tells them which deal to look at next, when to pass, and how to say no without burning the relationship.

## What we'd build together

- A vocabulary for the personal layer of dealflow (above your firm's CRM, below your brain).
- Templates for inbound triage, "no" notes, follow-up cadences.
- A small toolkit (probably Astro + content collections + a few agentic loops).

## Looking for

- 4–6 committed members who'll bring their actual dealflow practice (sanitized).
- One person willing to be the "lead curator" — collects the patterns, writes them up.

## How to join

Reach out via the FullStack VC Dojo. If we get four committed members by 2026-05-30, we kick off.
