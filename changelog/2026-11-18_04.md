## Why Care?

"Read the part about prompt injection" is easier to act on when the link drops you right there. Earlier today only recipe and handbook sections could be shared on their own. Now every h2, h3 and h4 the hub renders has an anchor, on tool pages, use cases, sessions and drafts too.

## What's New?

- **An anchor on every heading.** Headings with their own share page (h2 and h3 in published recipes and handbook chapters) keep the share icon, which sends that section's own preview card. Every other h2 to h4 gets a quieter link icon that sends `page#heading`.
- **The heading's name goes with it.** On a phone, either icon opens the share sheet with the heading as its title. On a laptop it copies the link and shows a toast naming the heading, so you know exactly what you copied.

## Under the Hood

The two kinds of anchor are one component (`HeadingAnchor.astro`) running one share helper, so they behave the same. The only difference is the preview: chat apps ignore the `#heading` part of a link, so a plain anchor unfurls with the page's card, while a section with its own `/s/` share page unfurls with a card titled for that section. Link icons used to be invisible until you hovered the heading; they now sit at low contrast so they can be found, and phones show them slightly stronger since there's no hover.
