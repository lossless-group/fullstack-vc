## Why Care?

A list of 68 releases is news; it isn't advice. Today's deck now says, month by month, what those releases let a fund do, and turns the personal-agents half of the session into a conversation instead of a lecture.

## What's New?

- **Every month slide leads with "Now you can…"** September, for example: ask any chat for a deck, doc or design; get agents that keep working between conversations; and use ChatGPT with PitchBook and FactSet built in. The releases sit underneath as the evidence.
- **The session in three parts:**
  1. A fast run through the updates, ending on the eight habits.
  2. Personal agents as a discussion: what worked, what let you down, how to make a win repeatable, and a five-minute show-and-tell.
  3. The home connector, with five steps to start this week.
- **Links that open in a new tab** on every slide that names a guide, so the deck stays put.
- **"Ditch PowerPoint" shows the proof:** cards for DidiDecks AI and MemoPop AI, the decks and memos we build this way for funds.
- **Habit 6 becomes "Give your AI direct access: APIs and your browser,"** with what browser access unlocks: sourcing lists, portfolio updates, fund-admin exports, data rooms, forms. It carries a caution on LinkedIn's scraping terms.
- **Claude Duet** (Oct 21) joins Dots as Anthropic's always-on agent, in the routine-work table and the personal-agents showcase.
- **New tools:**
  - [Claude Duet](/tools/claude-duet)
  - [Axel](/tools/axel), an AI travel agent; Michael now uses it.
  - [DidiDecks AI](/tools/dididecks-ai)
  - [MemoPop AI](/tools/memopop-ai)

## Under the Hood

- **Month takeaways live in the deck,** keyed by month, beside the shared timeline data, so the release list still can't drift from the timeline page.
- **A reveal-animation gotcha:** the deck's `[data-reveal]` styles override a container's own `display`, so the card grid sits inside the animated wrapper rather than on it.
