## Why Care?

Most hub links travel by phone. Until today, every one of them unfurled as the same Dojo illustration, whether you were sending a tool, a recipe or a session, so the person on the other end had no idea what you'd sent until they tapped.

Now each page unfurls as its own card: the tool's name and banner, the recipe's title, the handbook chapter's artwork. And when you want to point someone at one part of a long recipe, the share icon beside each heading sends a card titled with that section.

## What's New?

- **A card for every page.** 371 cards in the hub's own type and colors: a tall one for iMessage and WhatsApp, and a wide one for X and LinkedIn. Tools show their banner whole, in a rounded frame. Handbook chapters show their illustration. Recipes, sessions, people, projects and working groups each get their own title and summary.
- **A new site card** for the home page and anything without a card of its own: "Learn AI superpowers for your venture firm." The hand-made Dojo illustration stays as its wide image.
- **Share any section.** Every h2 and h3 in recipes and handbook chapters has a share icon. It sends a link whose preview is that section's own card, then lands the reader on the heading.
- **Share buttons.** An icon beside recipe and tool titles. On a phone they open the share sheet; on a laptop they copy the link and say so with a "Link copied" toast.

## How it Works

This follows the astro-knots blueprint [Share Every Page and Every Section With Its Own OG Image](https://github.com/lossless-group/astro-knots/blob/master/context-v/blueprints/Share-Every-Page-and-Section-With-Its-Own-OG-Image.md), first built for cathode-site and edviro-blog.

```text
share-cards.ts   every page and section → a card {kicker, title, subtitle, image}
og-render.ts     card → JPEG (satori → resvg → sharp), tall 1080×1440 and wide 1200×630
/og/{tall|wide}/{key}.jpg   static, written at build time
/s/{page}/{heading}/        a share page per section: its own card, then a redirect to page#heading
```

A page's card key is its own URL path, so the layout finds every page's card without each page having to name it. Chat apps ignore the `#heading` part of a link, which is why sections need their own `/s/` pages rather than fragments.

## Under the Hood

**A card renders once, not once per build.** The first build rendered 742 images and went from about 45 seconds to 217. So each card's JPEG is now cached under a hash of everything that determines it: its text, its image, the fonts, the logo and a version number. The next build took 10 seconds, and a normal deploy only renders the cards that actually changed. This went back into the blueprint as its own section.

**Banners have text in them.** A tool's og:image is usually a marketing banner with words on it, so cropping it full-bleed cut the words off. Tool cards set the banner as a rounded inset at its own proportions instead.

**Checked before shipping:** every built page has exactly one tall `og:image` of the right type and a wide `twitter:image`, every image file exists, every section button points at a share page that exists, and the cards still read when shrunk to a chat thumbnail and WhatsApp's square crop.
