## Why Care?

Anthropic and OpenAI each shipped dozens of things since our July session: new models, agents that keep working after you close the laptop, documents and spreadsheets made in a chat, and products built for finance. Nobody in a fund has time to read two companies' release notes. Now there's one page that lays it all out, month by month, with the source one click away.

## What's New?

- **[The timeline](/timelines/claude-and-chatgpt).** 65 releases from May 18 to Nov 18, 2026, with Anthropic and OpenAI in two columns. Each entry says what shipped, who can get it, and links to the company's own announcement or release note. The 13 that matter most for a fund are highlighted.
- **Filters and order.** Show only Claude apps, Claude Code, ChatGPT, Codex or Models; tick "Headlines only" for the short list; flip to newest first. `?order=newest` links straight to the flipped view.
- **[The Nov 19 deck](/slides/2026-11-19_monthly-all-hands).** "Agents at Work": a slide per month, the Claude and ChatGPT changes worth using in a fund, the defaults changing under you this week, and the seven kinds of artifact Claude now makes. It closes segment 1 with a takeaway for the bold: ditch PowerPoint and have Claude write the deck as code. The deck is the example; it's a web page.
- **Easy to find.** The timeline is in the More menu, the mobile menu and site search (search a release name like "Dots" or "Opus 5.5"), and it's linked from the session page.

## How it Works

The releases live in one data file, `src/data/claude-chatgpt-timeline-2026.json`. The timeline page and the deck's month slides both read it, so they can't disagree. Each entry carries a date, vendor, track, a short summary, plans, an importance from 1 to 3, and a source URL.

Research started with a Claude Code agent reading both companies' release notes and announcements, then went entry by entry against the primary pages. That changed several things people (including us) believed going in: Claude's memory across chats isn't new this year, but was rebuilt in July and shared with Cowork in August; OpenAI's Workspace Agents went generally available on May 21, not this month; and OpenAI's retirement FAQ moves Custom GPTs to plugins, not to Workspace Agents.

## Under the Hood

- **Filters without a framework.** The track chips and "Headlines only" toggle `hidden` on items, and months with nothing left disappear. The order button reverses the month sections and the items inside each lane in place. With JavaScript off, everything shows in date order.
- **Search.** The site's Pagefind index is built from explicit records, so the timeline gets one record whose text is every release title and summary.
- **Share card.** The page has its own tall and wide share images through the existing share-card registry.
- **What's left out.** Releases before May 18 (GPT-5.5, Codex on mobile, Claude Design) are outside the window; a few plan details neither company states are left blank rather than guessed.
