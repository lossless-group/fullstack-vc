## Why Care?

The personal-agents write-up names seven fellows and a dozen tools. Until now, finding out who George Babu is, or what Town does, meant clicking away from the sentence you were reading. Now you hover the name and the answer comes to you.

::image{src="https://ik.imagekit.io/xvpgfijuw/fullstack-vc/smart-link-hover-cards/Hover-Card__Person--George-Babu_20261119T021300Z.jpg" alt="A FullStack VC recipe in dark mode with a hover card open under the linked name George Babu, showing his photo, Aire Labs, Founder, 20 tools, Kauffman 18, and joined Jul 14, 2026"}

## What's New?

- **People.** Hover a fellow's name in any recipe, handbook chapter or tool page and their card opens: photo, firm, role, how many tools they track, Kauffman class and when they joined. The card links to their profile.
- **Tools.** Hover a tool's name and its card opens, with its banner, what it does, its tags and how many members use it, plus a link to the tool page.
- **Calm by design.** Cards open after a short pause, so skimming past names doesn't flash them; they sit above or below the name, whichever has room; and they stay open while you move into them. Keyboard focus opens them too, and Escape closes them.
- **Private stays private.** A member whose profile isn't public gets a plain link and no card.
- **Phones** don't hover, so a tap simply follows the link, as before.

## How it Works

The names are LFM wikilinks (`[[georgebabu|George Babu]]`, `[[tools/town|Town]]`). The site's wikilink resolver already decides where each one points; it now also tags people and tool links with a class, using an option LFM already has. When the markdown renderer meets a local link to `/people/…` or `/tools/…`, it draws a `HoverCard` instead of a plain link, using the same cards the people page and the tool catalogue use, filled in at build time. Nothing is fetched when you hover.

## Under the Hood

**Cards inside sentences.** These links sit in the middle of paragraphs, and a card is block markup. Put a `<div>` inside a `<p>` and the browser closes the paragraph early, splitting the sentence in two. So each link carries its card in a `<template>`, which HTML allows inside a paragraph and doesn't render. One small script clones the card into a single floating popover on hover. On the personal-agents post, all 29 hover cards stayed inside their paragraphs.

**The name.** In UI libraries this pattern is a *hover card* (Radix and shadcn call it `HoverCard`; GitHub calls its own "hovercards"). We kept the name, and kept it distinct from LFM's *link previews*, which unfurl external URLs inline. The pattern is now written up as a blueprint for every Astro Knots site, with a proposal to make link kinds a first-class part of LFM.
